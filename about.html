Every team deserves an analyst.

Lighthouse started in 2024 when our founders, then running a struggling meal-kit startup, realized they had six dashboards and zero answers. They had the data. What they lacked was someone to read it.

So they built the analyst instead: software that watches product data the way a good data scientist would, forms hypotheses, checks them, and writes up what it finds. The meal-kit company didn't make it. The tool that tried to save it did.

Today Lighthouse serves a few hundred product teams, most between two and two hundred people. We're a remote team of fourteen, profitable since 2025, and in no hurry to change either of those things.
