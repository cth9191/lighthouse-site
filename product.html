From raw events to written answers.

Here's what happens between "a user clicked something" and "you know what to build next."

Anomaly detection that respects your time

Lighthouse baselines every funnel and cohort, so it knows the difference between a normal Tuesday dip and a real problem. You only hear about changes that are statistically meaningful and big enough to matter.

Each alert arrives as a short written brief: what changed, which segment drove it, and what it correlates with.

A question box, not a query builder

Ask "where do trial users get stuck?" the way you'd ask a colleague. Lighthouse translates the question into analysis, runs it, and answers with a chart plus the reasoning it used, so you can check its work.

Follow-up questions keep the context. Drill from a trend to a segment to individual session paths without starting over.

Reports that write themselves

Every Monday, each team gets a digest of the week: what moved, why it moved, and one suggested focus. Investors love the monthly version; you'll stop dreading board-deck week.

Digests are editable before they send, so you stay in control of the narrative.

Works with what you already run

Native connections for Segment, Stripe, Postgres, BigQuery, and Snowflake, plus a lightweight snippet for everything else. Your data stays in your region and is never used to train shared models.
